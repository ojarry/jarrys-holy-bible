# Jarry's Holy Bible
**The complete King James Bible, in your hands, in Minecraft.**

Jarry's Holy Bible adds one simple, faithful item: a Holy Bible containing the **entire King James Version**, with all **66 books, 1,189 chapters and 31,102 verses**. Read Scripture by your campfire, in your base, or on a long voyage, without ever leaving the game.

## Reading
Right-click the Bible to open a clean, easy-to-use reader:
- Choose **Old Testament** or **New Testament**, then a **Book**, then a **Chapter**.
- Scroll through verses with the mouse wheel or the **Page Up / Page Down** buttons.
- **Prev / Next** moves chapter by chapter, even across books (Malachi 4 → Matthew 1).
- **Continue** picks up right where you left off during your session.
- Lightweight: the text is stored compressed, and each book loads only when you open it.

## Obtaining
There are four ways to get a Bible:
- **Your first join:** every player receives one Bible the first time they join a world. It's tracked per player, so it's never handed out twice, even after dying or relogging. If your inventory is full, it drops at your feet.
- **Crafting:** Book + Gold Ingot (shapeless).
- **Village chests:** a rare 0.1% find in every village chest. Loot is added through Fabric's loot API, so it plays nicely with other loot mods and data packs.
- **Creative:** in the Tools & Utilities tab.

The Bible stacks to 1 and can be held in your off hand.

## Requirements
You'll need **Fabric Loader** and **Fabric API**, installed on **both the client and the server**. Builds are available for Minecraft **26.3, 26.2, 26.1.2, 1.21.11 and 1.21.10**, so download the file that matches your game version.
